          How is this calculated?

          Train times come from the official LRT, MRT, Monorail, BRT, KTM Komuter and KLIA Ekspres/Transit timetables. Waiting is half the average gap between trains (at most 20 minutes), each change adds 5 minutes, and walking is estimated at 4.5 km/h along a route 60% longer than the straight line. Rapid KL buses can get you to a station if you choose Bus. Treat results as a guide, not a journey planner.
