      How is this calculated?

      Train times come from the official LRT, MRT, Monorail, BRT and KTM Komuter timetables. Waiting is half the average gap between trains, each change adds 5 minutes, and walking is estimated at 4.5 km/h in a straight line plus 30%. Buses and e-hailing are not included, so treat results as a guide.
